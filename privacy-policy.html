1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to Curtis Law, PLLC, an Arizona professional limited liability company, operating under the trade name Family Matters Legal ("we," "our," or "us"). We are the "data controller" of personal information collected through this Website.

Our registered office is: 123 N. Central Avenue, Suite 400, Phoenix, AZ 85004.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

a) Information You Provide Directly

  • Identity data: First name, last name
  • Contact data: Email address, telephone number, mailing address
  • Inquiry data: Information you provide in contact forms, describing your legal matter
  • Communications: Emails, phone call notes, and other correspondence with our office

b) Information Collected Automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system
  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, links clicked, referring URLs
  • Cookie data: See Section 7 for details

c) Sensitive Personal Information

Because we are a law firm, you may voluntarily share sensitive personal information in your inquiry, including details about legal matters, family situations, financial information, or health matters. We treat all such information with the highest degree of confidentiality. We do not request this information unless you choose to provide it.

3. How We Collect Information

  • Contact forms on our Website
  • Direct communications via email or phone
  • Cookies and similar technologies (see Section 7)
  • Analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, subject to your cookie preferences)

4. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiry or consultation request;
  • To evaluate whether we can assist with your legal matter;
  • To provide legal services to you as a client (subject to an engagement agreement);
  • To communicate with you about your matter or our services;
  • To comply with our legal and ethical obligations under Arizona Rules of Professional Conduct;
  • To improve our Website and services through analytics;
  • To send you information about our services, if you have consented or where permitted by law;
  • To comply with applicable laws and legal processes.

5. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted third parties who assist us in operating our Website, conducting business, or serving you (e.g., website hosting, email services, scheduling software), subject to confidentiality agreements;
  • Legal obligations: Where required by law, court order, or subpoena;
  • Professional obligations: Where required by the Arizona Rules of Professional Conduct;
  • With your consent: Where you have given us explicit consent to share your information;
  • Business transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of our practice, subject to the Arizona Rules of Professional Conduct regarding client files.

6. Attorney-Client Confidentiality

Once an attorney-client relationship is established, communications between you and our attorneys are protected by attorney-client privilege and the Arizona Rules of Professional Conduct, Rule 1.6 (Confidentiality of Information). We are ethically bound not to disclose confidential client information without your consent, except as permitted by Rule 1.6.

Important: Submitting a contact form or inquiry does not create an attorney-client relationship. Information submitted before an engagement agreement is signed is not protected by attorney-client privilege, though we treat it with professional discretion.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies on our Website. For full details, please see our Cookie Policy.

In summary, we use:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Essential for the Website to function;
  • Analytics cookies: To understand how visitors use our Website (e.g., Google Analytics);
  • Preference cookies: To remember your choices (such as cookie consent).

You can manage your cookie preferences through the cookie banner on our Website or in your browser settings.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit;
  • Access controls limiting who can access personal information;
  • Regular security assessments;
  • Staff training on data security and confidentiality.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you to take care when transmitting sensitive information.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Policy, including:

  • For prospective client inquiries: up to 12 months after last contact, unless a client relationship is established;
  • For client files: in accordance with Arizona Rules of Professional Conduct (typically a minimum of 5 years after matter closure, or as required by statute);
  • For analytics data: as determined by the analytics provider's settings (typically 26 months);
  • As required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

10. Your Rights Under Arizona Law (ACDPA)

Under the Arizona Consumer Data Privacy Act (A.R.S. § 18-551 et seq., effective December 31, 2025), Arizona residents have the following rights regarding their personal data:

  • Right to Know: You have the right to know whether we process your personal data;
  • Right to Access: You have the right to access your personal data that we hold;
  • Right to Correction: You have the right to correct inaccurate personal data;
  • Right to Deletion: You have the right to request deletion of your personal data (subject to exceptions);
  • Right to Data Portability: You have the right to receive your data in a portable format;
  • Right to Opt Out: You have the right to opt out of the sale of personal data (we do not sell personal data) and targeted advertising;
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 15. We will respond to verified requests within 45 days (extendable by an additional 45 days with notice).

Note: Some exceptions apply to personal data processed in connection with legal services, including information subject to attorney-client privilege or legal ethics rules.

11. Children's Privacy

This Website is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to delete such information.

12. Do Not Track Signals

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. Currently, our Website does not respond to DNT signals because there is no universally accepted standard for how websites should respond to such signals. We will revisit this policy as standards evolve.

13. External Links

Our Website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external sites you visit.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. If we make material changes, we will provide a more prominent notice. Your continued use of the Website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
