About Gavin

Gavin A. Curtis, Esq. — Dedicated to Your Family's Future

Gavin A. Curtis is an Arizona attorney with over 11 years of experience helping clients through some of life's most personal legal challenges. He founded Curtis Law, PLLC and created the Family Matters Legal brand around a single belief — that families deserve skilled, compassionate representation, not just legal processing.

Gavin is a skilled trial attorney who handles divorce and child custody litigation. He also helps families navigate probate — managing the distribution of assets with professionalism and sensitivity during difficult times. His practice also includes estate planning, helping clients protect what they've built for the people they love.

As the father of seven children, Gavin brings both legal expertise and genuine personal understanding to every family case. His focus is always on problem-solving and collaborative outcomes — reaching the best result with as little conflict as possible.
