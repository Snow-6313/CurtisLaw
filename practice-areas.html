Divorce & Legal Separation

Arizona is a no-fault divorce state. You do not need to prove wrongdoing — only that the marriage is "irretrievably broken." However, the process involves critical decisions about property, children, and finances that have lasting impacts on your life.

Whether your divorce is uncontested and straightforward, or involves complex assets and high conflict, your attorney provides clear-headed guidance and strong advocacy throughout every stage.

Child Custody & Parenting Time

Arizona law uses "legal decision-making" (formerly custody) and "parenting time" (formerly visitation). Courts always prioritize the best interests of the child under A.R.S. § 25-403.

We help parents build parenting plans that work — minimizing conflict, protecting your relationship with your children, and putting their wellbeing first.

Child Support

Arizona child support is calculated using the Income Shares Model, taking into account both parents' incomes, parenting time, healthcare, and childcare costs. The court follows the Arizona Child Support Guidelines.

We help ensure support orders are fair, accurate, and actually enforceable — and we can return to court when circumstances change.

Initial Support Orders Modification Requests Enforcement Actions Medical / Childcare Costs Arrears & Back Support

Spousal Maintenance (Alimony)

Arizona courts may award spousal maintenance (alimony) when one spouse lacks sufficient property to meet reasonable needs or is unable to be self-sufficient. Awards consider the length of marriage, standard of living, and earning capacity.

Whether you are seeking support or contesting it, we provide clear analysis and strong advocacy to protect your financial future.

Property Division

Arizona is a community property state. Generally, assets and debts acquired during the marriage are divided equally. However, the distinction between community and separate property can be complex — especially with business interests, investments, and retirement accounts.

We protect your fair share and ensure nothing is hidden, undervalued, or improperly categorized.

Real Estate Division Retirement & 401k (QDRO) Business Valuation Hidden Asset Discovery Separate Property Claims Debt Allocation

Paternity

Establishing legal paternity in Arizona gives fathers legal rights and children access to child support, insurance, and inheritance rights. Paternity can be established voluntarily or through court action.

We represent both mothers seeking to establish paternity for child support and fathers seeking to assert or contest their parental rights.

Prenuptial & Postnuptial Agreements

We draft clear, enforceable agreements that comply with the Arizona Uniform Premarital Agreement Act and protect both parties fairly.

Guardianship & Conservatorship

When a parent is unable to care for a child, or when an adult family member can no longer manage their own affairs, guardianship provides legal authority to step in and provide care and protection.

We handle guardianships for minors and adults, conservatorship proceedings, and court-supervised guardianships in Arizona Probate Court.
