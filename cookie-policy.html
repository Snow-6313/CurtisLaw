1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, improve performance, and provide information to the website owner.

Similar technologies include:

  • Local storage: Data stored in your browser that persists between sessions;
  • Session storage: Temporary data that is cleared when you close your browser tab;
  • Pixels and web beacons: Tiny images that record when a page is viewed;
  • Browser fingerprinting: Identifying your browser by its technical characteristics.

This policy covers all of these technologies collectively referred to as "cookies."

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure the Website functions properly (essential functions);
  • Remember your preferences and cookie consent choices;
  • Analyze how visitors use our Website to improve it;
  • Understand which pages and content are most useful to visitors.

We do not use cookies for advertising targeting, profiling, or selling your data to third parties.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the Website to function. They cannot be turned off. They do not store any personally identifiable information and are usually set in response to actions you take (like setting cookie preferences).

Analytics / Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our Website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. They help us improve the Website. These cookies are only set with your consent.

Preference / Functionality Cookies

These cookies allow the Website to remember choices you make (such as your preferred language or region) and provide enhanced, personalized features. These cookies are only set with your consent.

7. Browser Cookie Controls

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

Please note that restricting cookies may affect the functionality of this Website and many other websites you visit.

8. Opt-Out Links

  • Google Analytics: Install the Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on
  • Network Advertising Initiative: NAI Opt-Out Tool
  • Digital Advertising Alliance: DAA Opt-Out Tool

9. Impact of Disabling Cookies

Disabling or restricting cookies may affect your experience on this Website in the following ways:

  • Your cookie consent preference may not be remembered, and the banner may reappear on each visit;
  • We will not be able to analyze Website traffic or improve based on usage data;
  • Some interactive features may not function as expected.

Disabling cookies will not prevent you from accessing the Website's content or contacting us.
